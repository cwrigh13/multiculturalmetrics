Public library statistics

Last updated:  31 July 2025

Each year the State Library collects comprehensive statistics from NSW councils about their library services. 

Each year the State Library collects comprehensive statistics from NSW councils about their library services. 

The key indicators of public library use show that they are highly valued by their communities.

In 2023-24 there were:

  • 2.8 million members
  • 26.7 million physical visits to NSW public libraries
  • 39.7 million loans of collection items
  • 14 million virtual visits to public library websites
  • 11.3 million internet bookings (including wi-fi)

Source: NSW Public Library Statistics 2023-24

Public Libraries Statistics is an annual compilation of statistical data highlighting the usage and activities of public libraries in NSW.

NSW public library staff can use the Bibliostat Connect database to view historical statistics and additional statistics not included in the annual publication. You can also undertake benchmarking and comparisons with other library services. Passwords are provided to each NSW public library manager. 

Statistical data collection

Annual statistics are submitted online via Bibliostat Collect. Passwords are provided to each NSW public library manager.

Go to Bibliostat Collect

Note: If you have issues accessing this link contact your council IT department and ask them to modify your network security to allow access to collectconnect.baker-taylor.com on TCP/8880

Statistics collected on this form are used to compile the annual Public Library Statistics report. Selected data are also reported to the NSW Local Government Grants Commission, the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) and the National and State Libraries of Australasia (NSLA) to be used for reports on public libraries and local councils.

Statistical sampling periods

Sample survey periods provide library services with the ability to estimate annual information and customer service requests. Collecting and reporting data for this important indicator of use assists in demonstrating the value of public libraries.

NSW public libraries can use the enquiry completion rate (ECR) survey templates and forms to collect statistics for annual library service reporting.  

Download the ECR survey instructions below.

Enquiry completion rate survey instructions and definitions1 file(s)140.56 KB

Survey periods

The survey periods are set for seven calendar days, beginning on a Monday and concluding on a Sunday. This period ensures the full spread of library opening hours are included, whatever combination of hours or days a library facility is open to the public.
