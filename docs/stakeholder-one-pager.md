# Multicultural Metrics Dashboard — Stakeholder Summary

*Status: complete. Plain-language one-pager for Council and community partners.*

## What it is

A simple online tool for Georges River Libraries that brings together, in one place:

- **Where we stand against the standard.** A self-assessment of the library's maturity against the five areas of the *NSW Multicultural Library Service Benchmarks* (State Library of NSW, 2024), with the reasoning recorded and tracked over time.
- **What our programs deliver.** Activity figures — programs run, attendance, new members — that can be viewed by community, suburb, and time period.

These two are shown separately and clearly labelled. Activity numbers describe what we did; they are not a benchmark score.

## The problem it addresses

Evaluating services to culturally and linguistically diverse (CALD) communities has been manual and scattered, and hard to compare from year to year. The dashboard makes that evaluation consistent, transparent, and honest about its sources, so the library's position can be seen at a glance and shared without rebuilding a report each time.

## What it does well

- Shows movement against the benchmark across reporting cycles, with the evidence behind each judgement.
- Gives management, Council, and partners a single transparent view.
- Represents the benchmark standard exactly as published — no invented targets, every figure attributed to its source.

## What it deliberately does not do

- It does not pull live data; figures are updated by hand.
- It does not hold any personal or identifiable information — aggregate counts only.
- It does not reduce the work of collecting data, only the work of presenting it.
- It has no automated or AI analysis.

## Status

A working draft. Some local figures (e.g. census and collection data) and two further standards (ALIA, IFLA) are still being sourced and are marked as unconfirmed until obtained. No figure is presented as final while it carries that marker.
